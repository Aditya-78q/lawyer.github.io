Areas of Practice

The following are the areas in which Advocate Durga Sagar Babu Bangaru practices at the Andhra Pradesh High Court.

Civil Rights

Matters relating to the fundamental rights and civil liberties of citizens, including writ petitions and public interest litigation before the High Court.

Business Law

Matters relating to commercial disputes, contracts, and corporate compliance before the courts of law.

Family Law

Matters relating to matrimonial disputes, maintenance, custody, and related family law proceedings.

Criminal Law

Matters relating to criminal proceedings before the Andhra Pradesh High Court, including bail applications, revisions, and appeals.

Public Interest Litigation

Filing and arguing Public Interest Litigations before the Andhra Pradesh High Court on matters of public concern.

Advocate Durga Sagar

Advocate enrolled with the Bar Council of Andhra Pradesh (Enrolment No. AP/705/2020). Practicing at the Andhra Pradesh High Court since November 2020.
