Advocate Durga Sagar Babu Bangaru

Advocate Durga Sagar Babu Bangaru is an enrolled advocate practicing at the Andhra Pradesh High Court, Amaravati. His areas of practice include Criminal Law, Civil Rights, and Public Interest Litigation.

In addition to legal practice, he serves as Head of 7 Times News Channel, contributing to public legal awareness through media.

Areas of Practice

The following are the areas in which Advocate Durga Sagar Babu Bangaru practices before the Andhra Pradesh High Court.

Civil Rights

Matters relating to fundamental rights and civil liberties of citizens, including writ petitions before the High Court.

Public Interest Litigation

Filing and arguing Public Interest Litigations before the Andhra Pradesh High Court on matters of public concern.

Criminal Law

Appearances in criminal proceedings before the AP High Court, including bail matters, revisions, and appeals.

Legal Awareness

Contributing to public awareness of legal rights and remedies through media platforms.

Media Law

Matters relating to media regulations, free speech, and press-related legal issues.
